What you get out of it.

  • Build a real car

    From concept sketches to competition grids, every GARUDA car is designed, machined and driven by undergraduates.

  • Race internationally

    The team has represented India at Shell Eco-marathon Asia for over a decade.

  • Go further after

    Many alumni go on to lead engineering teams at global automotive, aerospace and energy companies.

Four disciplines, one car.

We recruit across the whole engineering stack. You don’t need to be from mechanical to join the mechanical team, and we welcome first-years.

  • Mechanical

    Chassis, suspension, aerodynamics, powertrain, manufacturing, composites.

  • Electrical

    Battery and BMS, motor and drives, embedded systems, data acquisition, harnessing.

  • Software and Telemetry

    Vehicle simulation, live telemetry, lap analysis, driver feedback tools.

  • Management and Ops

    Sponsorship, finance, logistics, social media, event coordination.
