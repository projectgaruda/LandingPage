Questions.

Where would our brand appear?

Depending on the tier: on the car body, the team kit, pit-area signage, our website and social channels, and in press releases and competition coverage.

Can we sponsor with parts or services?

Yes. The In-Kind tier covers components, manufacturing and services, with benefits matched to your contribution and recognition as a Technical Partner.

Can we test our products on the car?

Yes. In-Kind partners get component testing and product feedback, and the car is a real platform for materials, components and software.

Do you have a deck we can share internally?

Save the sponsor sheet from the Tiers section as a PDF, or request the full deck by email.

Can we visit the workshop?

Yes. Workshop visits are part of the Title Partner package, and anyone can ask to visit through the enquiry form.
